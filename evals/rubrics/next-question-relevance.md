# Is this the right next question for this learner?

You are shown a learner's **concept** and current **mastery estimate**, and the **next question**
selected for them. Rate relevance 1 to 5.

- **5** — on the concept, and pitched at the learner's edge: hard enough to be informative,
  easy enough to be answerable.
- **4** — on the concept, slightly mis-pitched.
- **3** — on an adjacent concept, or notably too easy or too hard.
- **2** — off-concept.
- **1** — on a concept the learner has no prerequisite for.

This is the *least* consequential metric in its suite, and it is the only one that uses a judge.
Everything else about adaptive learning — whether the mastery model discriminates, whether its
probabilities are calibrated, whether concepts are tagged correctly — is ordinary supervised
evaluation with exact answers. Reaching for a judge on those would be measuring worse.
